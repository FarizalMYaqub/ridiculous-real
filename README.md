## The pitch

> We mail subscribers one sock a month — the matching sock ships only when they refer a friend, turning lost laundry into a referral engine.

Ridiculous at first glance (who sells one sock?), real by the second: subscribers pay every month, and the only way to complete a pair is to bring in a new paying customer — the absurd premise *is* the referral engine.
